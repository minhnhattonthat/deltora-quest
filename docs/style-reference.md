## Layout
- Fill pages: two lines of up to ~45 characters before adding `{p}`; break at phrase boundaries.
- The Japanese `{sp}` word gaps are dropped. Inner thoughts stay in (parentheses), as in the JP.
- Ellipses `...` with no spaces before; `!?` for surprised questions.
