# Synopsis (for translators)

The game retells the first Deltora Quest series (Emily Rodda, 8 novels) with game-only side places. Names and
terms: `glossary.md` (official novel spellings). The extracted script decides the wording; this file gives the order,
who is who, and the reveals translators must not spoil early.

## Who is who
- **Lief**: 16, son of Jarred and Anna, the blacksmiths of Del. Brave, impulsive; the player.
- **Barda**: big, gruff, formerly a palace guard; posed as a beggar outside the smithy for years. Lief's protector.
- **Jasmine**: wild girl from the Forest of Silence, raised alone; talks to animals; companions **Kree** (black bird,
  "Caw!"), **Filli** (small furry creature). Blunt, distrustful of towns.
- **Jarred** and **Anna**: in the prologue Jarred is King **Endon**'s boyhood friend; Lief knows his parents as the
  blacksmiths Jarred and Anna. Lief's letters home are addressed "Father" (父さん).
- **Endon**: the weak young king who lost the Belt; **Sharn** his queen. **Prandine**: chief advisor, secretly the
  Shadow Lord's servant. **The Shadow Lord** (影の大王): the enemy from the Shadowlands beyond the mountains.
- **Grey Guards**: the Shadow Lord's soldiers (game: 影の憲兵 "shadow military police" = Grey Guards).
- **Doom** (this game also calls him Joker / ジョーカー, as the Japanese anime does): scarred leader of the Resistance.
- **Dain**: young Resistance member who joins them (says he is from Tora). Late reveal: he is an Ol (shape-shifter)
  serving the Shadow Lord. Keep his early lines warm and innocent.
- **Final reveals (novel canon; follow the script's timing, never hint earlier):** the blacksmith couple who raised
  Lief are really Endon and Sharn in disguise, so **Lief is the heir** of Deltora. The real Jarred and Anna fled in
  their place; Anna died, Jarred lost his memory and became **Doom**, and **Jasmine is his daughter**.

## Unsure (check the script, ask the controller)
Some game-only names in chapters 1, 3 and 7 are not in the novels (Mokito, Mikita, Lucas, Ichabod...). Treat them as
game-coined: keep the katakana sound in natural English spelling and report them as NEW TERM.
